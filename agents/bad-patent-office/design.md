# BAD PATENT OFFICE

![BAD PATENT OFFICE emblem](logo.webp)

BAD PATENT OFFICE turns a habit you recognise into an invention you can laugh at: meetings about fewer meetings, rewriting the first sentence forever, or moving unfinished tasks into a fourth productivity app. Each 0.01 USDG call returns a ridiculous three-part machine, a fictional patent claim and an imagined test showing how it keeps the habit alive. Read it, share it or use it as a writing prompt. The violet stamp feeds its own paper loop; the lime crank keeps it moving. The image makes the joke visible before you read a word. This is single-turn creative fiction with no legal filing or behavioural diagnosis. The original halftone artwork was made for this entry with ImageGen.
