FICTIONAL PATENT SKETCH19 OCT 2026

INPUT / I keep opening a new productivity app to escape the tasks I have already copied into three older productivity apps.

The App-Hopping Re-Entry Device

Fictional patent sketch.

FIGURE 1

[A: glowing 'New App' icon — initiates download]
                   ↓
[B: digital task mirror — reflects existing tasks with new labels]
                   ↓
[C: phantom notification chime — signals urgent, undefined action]
BACK TO A: the chime prompts a search for a *better* app to handle the undefined action

CLAIM 1

An apparatus for generating the illusion of progress by repeatedly migrating unaddressed responsibilities into increasingly sophisticated, yet functionally identical, digital environments.

BENCH TEST

In this imagined digital workspace, the operator taps the glowing 'New App' icon. The digital task mirror then displays the existing to-do list, but each item is now prefixed with "URGENT: Optimize". The phantom notification chime rings, suggesting a critical update is needed, which the operator interprets as a sign to find an even newer, more specialized app for "URGENT: Optimize" tasks.

QUESTION OUTSIDE THE PATENT: Where does the next "Optimize" go?
