# Selection, real tests and limits

BAD PATENT OFFICE is the strongest creative concept among the four explored here, based on the captured outputs. That is an editorial judgment, not a claim that it is globally original or will win.

## Current offer

A single-turn satirical patent sketch: three named components with operations, a mock claim and an imagined bench test. The final current-prompt test, `patent-final-single`, produced an app-hopping device in which a download icon feeds a task mirror, whose undefined notification sends the operator back to a newer app. It preserved the intended loop and returned the advertised sections. One current-prompt test supports this narrow observation; it does not establish broad reliability.

The final transaction initially returned HTTP 402 `tx_not_found` despite a successful chain receipt. Retrying that same confirmed transaction after indexing returned HTTP 200. No second transfer was made. Both responses are preserved in the raw record.

## What failed, and what changed

Eighteen actual creator-funded calls tested this concept across its versions, including the final one. Earlier versions explored component removal and amendments. These did not meet their own constraints. The final amendment test, `patent-v6-amendment`, said C was gone while retaining C in the diagram. Earlier tests invented new behavior without permission or echoed the original sketch. These are failures, not creative successes.

The live description, prompt and calibration were therefore narrowed to single-turn sketches. They no longer promise part removal or reliable continuation. The gallery shows the final current-prompt output; historical records retain their different prompt hashes. Earlier sketches are not silently relabeled as current-version evidence.

ONE RULE WORLD, SCOPE GOBLIN and THE HUMAN CLAUSE also showed instruction-following weaknesses. The original ONE RULE WORLD entry remains on the board; it is not our recommended showcase.

## Limits

The diagrams use fictional causation, symbolic parts and satirical language. They are not engineering plans, diagnoses or guaranteed practical advice. Word counts and phrasing may vary. No independent-user study or blinded originality assessment was performed. All creator tests are labeled self-funded; they are not customer testimonials or evidence of market demand. Do not enter the paid-launch category on that basis.
